## Scope

- Press start: each step (bloom, each pour, valve open or close) appears one at a time
  with a countdown and the running total to pour to.
- Big numbers and buttons readable at arm's length; the screen stays awake.
- When it ends, the brew time fills in the brew log.
- He can skip the timer and just log.

**Not in this plan:** rating the brew (`1.x-ratings.md`).
