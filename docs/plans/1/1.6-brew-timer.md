# Plan: Guided brew timer

**Status:** improved, not built
**Outline:** `docs/outlines/coffee-tracker.md`, sections "The first version" (Guided brew timer), "Look and feel"
**Depends on:** `1.5-brew-log.md` (must be built first: the **Brew time** box and **Save
brew** on `BrewScreen.tsx`), and through it `1.4-brew-setup.md` (`brewSetup.ts`,
`brewRows`), `1.3-recipes.md` (`RecipeStep`, `clock.ts`) and `1.1-look-and-sign-in.md`

## Goal

Add a **Start timer** button to the brew screen. Pressing it fills the phone with one
step at a time: "Bloom, pour to 27 g", a big countdown to the next step, then "Pour to
135 g", "Open the valve" and so on, each with the running total to pour to. Numbers and
buttons are big enough to read at arm's length with wet hands, and the screen doesn't go
dark while it runs. When the coffee has drained he taps **Done**: the timer's time goes
into the **Brew time** box, and he saves the brew as in 1.5. The timer is optional: he
can still type a time, or none, and save. Nothing new is saved and no database change.
Ratings are 1.7.

## Decisions (settled)

Answered 2026-11-18: every recommendation below was approved, and the plan follows it.

1. **A sound when the step changes.** Recommended: **a single soft tone** when the timer
   moves to the next step by itself and when the target time is reached, plus a short
   buzz on phones that can (Android; iPhones don't allow it from a website). He is
   looking at the scale, not the phone. On an iPhone the silent switch mutes it. The
   alternative is a silent timer (one line removed). (Steps 3, 5.)
2. **After Done.** Recommended: Done goes **back to the brew screen** with the brew time
   filled in, and he taps **Save brew** (one more tap, but he can check the time and
   settings first). The alternative saves the brew the moment he taps Done. (Step 6.)
3. **Does the timer stop at the recipe's target time?** Recommended: **no**. After the
   last step it counts down to the target ("0:35 to target"), then on past it ("0:12
   over"), and stops only when he taps **Done**, so the logged time is what it really
   took (as 1.5 decided). The alternative stops at the target and logs the target time.
   (Steps 2, 5.)

The timer follows 1.3's recommendation: a step with an "at" time starts at that time; the
step after the bloom pour starts once the bloom time has passed; any other step without
a time waits for a tap on **Next**.

## What he sees

**The brew screen (changed from 1.5):**

- Above the **Brew time** box, a large **Start timer** button (outlined in the `line`
  colour, ink text, at least 56px tall). **Save brew** stays the copper main button.
- If any box on the screen shows a problem: "Fix the boxes marked above first." and the
  timer doesn't start (the same sentence as **Save brew**).
- While a timer is running and he has come back to this screen (Back, or the phone's back
  gesture): a slim line at the top, "Timer running · 1:23", with **Back to timer**.
  Changing the dose here doesn't change the running timer's weights.
- After **Done**: the **Brew time** box shows the time ("3:10"), the page scrolled so
  **Save brew** is in view. Pressing **Start timer** again starts over from 0:00.

**The timer (same address plus `?timer=on`, the rest of the brew screen hidden):**

- Top row, small and muted: the time since Start ("1:13") on the left, the recipe name
  on the right.
- The middle, centred, the biggest things on any screen:
  - The step's name in the serif: "Bloom" (the first pour, when the recipe has a bloom
    time), "Pour", or the step's own words ("Open the valve").
  - For a pour, the running total huge ("**135 g**", at least 72px, tabular numbers), and
    the single amount small and muted beside it ("+108").
  - The countdown to the next step, large ("0:32"), muted label "until next step".
  - "Next: Pour to 270 g" (or "Next: Open the valve") in muted text.
- A step with no time waits: instead of a countdown, the time since it began counts up,
  muted, and the **Next** button is how he moves on.
- On the last step, the countdown's place shows the recipe's target instead ("0:35 to
  target", then "0:12 over target" in muted text). No target: the time since that step
  began, counting up.
- At the bottom, at least 56px tall and well apart:
  - **Next** (outlined): moves to the next step now, even before its time. Hidden on the
    last step.
  - **Done** (copper, the main action): stops the timer, fills the brew time, back to the
    brew screen.
  - **Cancel timer** (a small muted link, set apart from Done): stops the timer, fills
    nothing, back to the brew screen.
- A recipe with no pours and no steps: just the time since Start, the target line, and
  **Done**.
- Where the browser can't keep the screen awake: one muted line, "Your phone may dim the
  screen during the brew." The timer still keeps true time (below).
- The screen stays on while the timer runs, and goes back to normal sleep after Done or
  Cancel.

**Keeps time while the phone sleeps or he switches apps:** coming back shows the right
time and the right step; steps whose time passed meanwhile have been moved through.

**Refreshing during the timer** returns to the brew screen with the recipe's settings,
timer stopped (as 1.4 says for any refresh).

**Desktop:** the same timer in the centred column from 1.1.

## Data

- **Nothing new is saved, and no new tables.** No migration, so the next migration number
  stays `005-…`.
- The timer's start time and steps live only on the open brew screen (not in browser
  storage), so no Export/Import is needed. The time reaches the database only through 1.5's
  **Save brew** (`brew_seconds`), exactly as if he had typed it.
- `?timer=on` in the address only says which view is showing; it carries nothing private.

## Steps

### 1. Pull and confirm 1.5 is built

Pull `main`. Confirm `src/coffee/BrewScreen.tsx` has the **Brew time** box and **Save
brew**, and `src/coffee/brewSetup.ts` (`BrewDraft`, `brewRows`), `src/coffee/brewLog.ts`
(`brewToSave`) and `src/coffee/clock.ts` exist. If not, stop: 1.5 (and what it needs)
come first. Note the exact shape `brewRows` returns (row kinds and field names); step 2
uses it as it is.

*Check:* `npm run check` passes before any change.

### 2. Rules (pure functions, tested)

`src/coffee/brewTimer.ts`. Times are whole seconds since Start.

- `TimerStep` (hand-written): the `brewRows` row it came from (pour with running total
  and single amount, or action with text), a `title` ("Bloom", "Pour" or the action's
  text), and when it starts: `at` (seconds from Start) or `after` (seconds after the
  step before it began), or neither (waits for **Next**).
- `timerSteps(rows, bloomSeconds)`: one `TimerStep` per row, in order. The first pour is
  titled "Bloom" when `bloomSeconds` is set. A row's own `at` always wins. The row right
  after the first pour, when it has no `at` and a bloom is set, gets `after =
  bloomSeconds`.
- `startTimer(steps)`: the begun list `[0]` (the first step begins at Start, whatever its
  `at`), or `[]` with no steps.
- `nextStartTime(steps, begun)`: when the next step should begin: its `at`, or the last
  begun time plus its `after`, or `null` (waits for a tap, or there is no next step).
- `advanceTimer(steps, begun, elapsed)`: while the next step's start time is known and
  has been reached, add it to the begun list **at that start time** (not at `elapsed`), so
  after the phone slept, several steps catch up at once and a bloom counted from them
  stays exact. Returns the same list when nothing changed.
- `tapNext(steps, begun, elapsed)`: the next step begins now (`elapsed` added); unchanged
  on the last step.
- `timerView(steps, begun, elapsed, totalSeconds)`: the current step (or none), the next
  step (or none), `countdown` to the next step (or `null`), `sinceStep` (seconds since the
  current step began), and on the last step `toTarget` (target minus elapsed; negative
  means over; `null` without a target).
- `elapsedSeconds(startedAtMs, nowMs)`: whole seconds, rounded down, never below 0.

*Check:* `npx vitest run src/coffee/brewTimer.test.ts` passes.

### 3. Keep the screen awake, and the tone

Both in `src/lib/` (nothing coffee-specific, other sections may want them):

- `src/lib/useWakeLock.ts`: `useWakeLock(on)` asks the browser to keep the screen on
  (`navigator.wakeLock.request('screen')`) while `on` is true; asks again when the page
  becomes visible after being hidden (the browser drops it then); lets go when `on`
  turns false or the screen closes. Returns whether it is supported, for the "may dim"
  line. Failures are ignored quietly (the timer still works).
- `src/lib/chime.ts` (decision 1): `unlockChime()` creates the browser's sound
  player inside the **Start timer** tap (phones only allow sound after a tap);
  `chime()` plays one soft sine tone, about 0.3 s, fading out, and calls
  `navigator.vibrate?.(150)`. Does nothing if sound isn't available.

*Check:* `npm run check` passes (no tests: these only talk to the browser).

### 4. The timer's state

`src/coffee/useBrewTimer.ts`, a hook used by `BrewScreen`:

- Holds `null` (no timer) or `{ startedAtMs, steps, begun, totalSeconds }`.
- `start(rows, bloomSeconds, totalSeconds)`: `unlockChime()`, `timerSteps`, `startTimer`,
  `startedAtMs = Date.now()`. The steps are fixed at Start (changing the dose afterwards
  doesn't change them).
- While running: re-checks every 250 ms. Elapsed always comes from `elapsedSeconds(start,
  Date.now())`, never from counting ticks, so a sleeping phone loses nothing. Each check
  runs `advanceTimer`; if the begun list grew, or the target was just reached, and the
  timer view is showing, `chime()` once.
- `next()`: `tapNext` (no tone). `done()`: returns the elapsed seconds and clears the
  timer. `cancel()`: clears it.
- `useWakeLock(timer !== null)`.
- Leaving the brew screen ends the hook with it: the timer is gone, as 1.4 says.

*Check:* `npm run check` passes.

### 5. The timer view

`src/coffee/BrewTimer.tsx`, shown only by `BrewScreen`, gets `timerView(...)`, the
elapsed time, the recipe name, whether the screen can be kept awake, and `onNext`,
`onDone`, `onCancel`. It shows what "The timer" above lists. Sizes: running total at
least 72px serif tabular, countdown at least 48px, buttons at least 56px with clear space
between **Next** and **Done**; colours by job name only (`paper`, `ink`, `muted`,
`line`, `accent`, `on-accent`). The countdown and pour numbers use `formatClock` and
whole grams; no animation on the numbers (the 1.1 screen fade only).

*Check:* on `npm run dev`, the worked-example recipe in step 6's check shows each part.

### 6. Wire it into the brew screen

In `src/coffee/BrewScreen.tsx`:

- **Start timer** above the Brew time box. If any box has a problem, show the "Fix the
  boxes marked above first." sentence, as **Save brew** does. Otherwise
  `start` with the rows from `brewRows(draft)`, `draft.bloomSeconds` and
  `draft.totalSeconds` (names as step 1 found them) and open the view by adding `timer=on` to the address with
  `useSearchParams` from `react-router` (a new history entry, so Back returns to the
  brew screen with the draft intact). The route itself doesn't change.
- `?timer=on` with a running timer shows `BrewTimer` instead of the rest of the screen.
  `?timer=on` with no timer (after a refresh, or a pasted address) replaces itself with
  the plain brew screen address.
- A running timer with the view closed: the "Timer running · 1:23 · Back to timer" line;
  **Back to timer** adds `timer=on` again.
- **Done** (decision 2): `done()`, set the Brew time box to `formatClock(seconds)`,
  go back one history entry (to the brew screen), then scroll **Save brew** into view.
  **Cancel timer**: `cancel()`, go back one entry, Brew time untouched.
- The draft stays on this screen: no shared holder needed (see Notes).

*Check:* on `npm run dev`, set up the recipe below, start the timer, wait for the bloom
to move on by itself, tap **Next** through the rest, wait 10 seconds, **Done**: Brew time
shows the time and **Save brew** saves it (History shows the same time).

### 7. Publish

Run `npm run check`, commit (files staged by name), push `main`.

## Notes

- **The timer is a view of the brew screen, not its own screen with a shared holder**
  (rejected: a separate route plus a React context around the coffee routes, as 1.4 and
  1.5 suggested for that case). The draft, the Brew time box and **Save brew** already
  live on the brew screen, so the timer only has to hand back one number. A context
  would add a provider, a guard for opening the timer address with no draft, and a
  second place the draft could go stale.
- **`?timer=on` in the address** (rejected: a plain on/off switch on the screen). The
  phone's back gesture is easy to trigger with wet hands; without a history entry it
  would leave the brew screen and lose the dose and the running timer. With it, Back
  just closes the timer view. A search value, not a new route, so the same screen stays
  open and keeps its state.
- **The timer keeps running when the view is closed**, rejected: Back cancelling it. An
  accidental swipe mid-brew shouldn't lose the time.
- **Time from the clock, not from counting ticks:** browsers slow or pause timers on a
  locked phone or a hidden tab. Working out elapsed from the start time each check is
  always right after waking. Steps reached while asleep are given their scheduled start
  time, so a bloom counted from them stays exact.
- **"after" for the bloom, "at" for everything else** (1.3's recommendation). Counting
  the bloom from when the bloom pour began, not from 0:00, also works when he taps
  **Next** early or late.
- **The first step begins at Start**, even if it has an "at" time: he taps Start when he
  is ready to pour. Rejected: a "get ready" countdown before the first step (more state,
  not asked for).
- **No pause button:** coffee doesn't pause. Rejected as clutter.
- **Steps fixed at Start:** rebuilding them while running would move weights under him.
- **Wake lock in `src/lib/`**, via the browser's Screen Wake Lock (free, built in; recent
  iPhones and Android). Rejected: a "keep awake" library playing a hidden video (an extra
  package and a battery cost for the few browsers without wake lock). It needs a secure
  address, which the live site and `localhost` are.
- **Tone made in the browser**, no sound file (nothing to download or host). Phones only
  allow sound after a tap, so it is unlocked by the **Start timer** tap.
- **Over 60 minutes:** `parseClock` refuses brew times over 60:00, so a forgotten timer's
  time shows 1.5's sentence under the Brew time box and he types the real one. Rejected: a
  special case for something that shouldn't happen.
- **Rejected for now:** a progress ring (decoration), showing past steps on the timer
  (one step at a time, per the outline), changing the dose from the timer view, a
  setting to turn the tone off (decision 1 decides on or off for everyone).
- **For later plans:**
  - Nothing here touches the database; the next migration is still `005-…` (1.7).
  - 1.7: after **Done** and **Save brew**, home shows "Brew saved."; the "rate me" card
    goes there as 1.5 planned. The timer doesn't change that path.
  - 1.8: a "repeat" brew opens the same brew screen, so it gets the timer for free; the
    draft must still carry `bloomSeconds` and `totalSeconds` for the timer.
  - 1.9: nothing to back up from this plan.
  - `useWakeLock` and `chime` in `src/lib/` are free for other sections (a reading timer,
    say).
