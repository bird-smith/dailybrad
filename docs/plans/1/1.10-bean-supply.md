# Plan: Cups left and cost per cup

**Status:** stub, not written yet
**Outline:** `docs/outlines/coffee-tracker.md`, sections "The first version" (Beans), "Rules and worked example"
**Depends on:** `1.5-brew-log.md` (the `brews` table); split out of it to keep it under
400 lines

## Scope

- Each bag in use on the Beans screen quietly shows about how many cups are left and its
  cost per cup, for example "About 8 cups left · $1.16 a cup".
- The outline's rules: cups left is bag size minus all doses logged from it, divided by
  the dose, rounded down; cost per cup is bag cost divided by bag size, times the dose.
  Worked example: 340 g bag at $22, 18 g dose: $1.16 a cup; after ten 18 g brews, 160 g
  and 8 cups left.

  request by default.
