# Plan: Cups left and cost per cup

**Status:** improved, not built
**Outline:** `docs/outlines/coffee-tracker.md`, sections "The first version" (Beans), "Rules and worked example"
**Depends on:** `1.5-brew-log.md` (the `brews` table); split out of it to keep it under
400 lines. Also uses 1.2's Beans screen and `formatMoney`, 1.7's `getLatestBrew`, and
1.8's database-function pattern (its migration 007 comes first).

## Goal

Each bag in use on the Beans screen quietly shows about how many cups are left in it and
what one cup costs, for example "About 8 cups left · $1.16 a cup", so he can see when to
buy more and what a bag really costs per brew. Nothing new is typed or saved: it is
worked out from the bag's size and cost and the doses he has logged from it.

## Decisions (settled)

Answered 2026-11-18: every recommendation below was approved, and the plan follows it.

1. **Which dose "cups left" divides by.** The outline says "today's dose", but the Beans
   screen has no "today". Recommended: the dose of the **last brew from that bag**; no
   brews from it yet: his last brew of any bag; no brews at all: only "340 g left". The
   alternative, a fixed dose he types per bag, is one more box to fill in. (Step 3.)
2. **Where it shows.** Recommended: on the Beans screen's **bags in use only**. The
   alternative also puts "About 8 cups left" on the home screen's bean card: handy for
   knowing when to buy, but one more thing on the calmest screen. If he wants it, step 5
   also loads the supply on home and adds the cups part (no cost) under the roast line.

## What he sees

**Beans screen (`#/coffee/beans`), bags in use:** under each row's roast age, one small
muted line:

- "About 8 cups left · $1.16 a cup"
- "About 1 cup left · $1.16 a cup", and "Less than a cup left · $1.16 a cup" once fewer
  grams are left than one dose, including when more has been logged than the bag held (it
  never shows a negative amount).
- No bag size: no line at all (neither sum can be done).
- No cost: only the cups part, "About 8 cups left".
- No brews logged at all yet, from any bag: "340 g left".
- Finished bags: nothing, as before.

While the sums load, the line's space stays empty (no flicker or spinner); the bean list
itself shows as soon as it has loaded. If the sums fail to load, the rows show without the
line, and one muted "Can't load cups left right now." with **Try again** sits under the
list. The list, **Use** and **Add bean** keep working.

The figures update each time the Beans screen opens: after a brew, opening Beans shows one
dose fewer.

**Desktop:** the same screen in the centred column; nothing changes.

**Phone and computer:** both show the same figures, since they come from the online
database.

## Data

- **Nothing new is saved and no table changes.** Migration `008-bean-supply.sql` adds one
  read-only database function, `bean_supply`, that returns, for each bag in use with at
  least one brew: the total dose logged from it and the dose of its newest brew, both in
  tenths of a gram (whole numbers).
- It runs **as the person asking** (`security invoker`), so the locks on `brews` and
  `beans` apply: it only ever sees his own rows. Signed-out visitors may not run it.
- Why a function: adding up doses in the site would mean downloading every brew from
  every bag in use, and Supabase hands over at most 1000 rows per request. A bag he never
  marks finished would quietly undercount and show more cups than he has. The function
  returns one row per bag in use, however many brews.
- The fallback dose (his newest brew of any bag) comes from 1.7's `getLatestBrew()`, one
  row.
- Nothing in browser storage, so no Export/Import here. Nothing new is stored, so 1.9's
  backup file is unchanged (1.9's notes say the same); a restored backup's brews count
  straight away.

### 2. Migration 008 and hand batch A

Write `supabase/migrations/008-bean-supply.sql`:

```sql
-- Dose totals per bag in use, in tenths of a gram, for "cups left".
-- Runs as the caller, so the "own rows only" locks on brews and beans apply.
create function bean_supply()
returns table (
  bean_id uuid,
  used_tenths integer,
  last_dose_tenths integer
)
language sql stable security invoker
set search_path = ''
as $$
  select b.bean_id,
         (sum(b.dose_grams) * 10)::integer,
         ((array_agg(b.dose_grams order by b.brewed_at desc))[1] * 10)::integer
  from public.brews b
  join public.beans n on n.id = b.bean_id and n.user_id = b.user_id
  where b.user_id = auth.uid()
    and not n.finished
  group by b.bean_id;
$$;

-- New functions can be run by everyone unless told otherwise.
revoke execute on function bean_supply() from public, anon;
grant execute on function bean_supply() to authenticated;
```

Hand batch A (click-by-click, `.claude/skills/click-by-click/SKILL.md`): **SQL Editor →
New query**, paste the file, **Run**.

*Check:* "Success. No rows returned", and **Database → Functions** lists `bean_supply`.

### 3. Rules (pure functions, tested)

- `src/coffee/types.ts` (add): `BeanSupply` matching the function's columns
  (`bean_id`, `used_tenths`, `last_dose_tenths`), hand-written.
- `src/coffee/beanSupply.ts`. All grams in **tenths** (whole numbers), so doses like
  18.1 g never drift; money in whole cents.
  - `toTenths(grams)`: `Math.round(grams * 10)`, for `getLatestBrew`'s `dose_grams`
    (18.1 arrives as a number; times 10 is not exactly 181 without rounding).
  - `doseFor(beanId, supply, latestDoseTenths)`: that bag's `last_dose_tenths`; no row
    for it: `latestDoseTenths`; neither: `null` (decision 1).
  - `cupsLeft(bagGrams, usedTenths, doseTenths)`: grams left is `bagGrams * 10 -
    usedTenths`; 0 or less gives 0; otherwise left divided by the dose, rounded down.
  - `costPerCupCents(costCents, bagGrams, doseTenths)`: `costCents * doseTenths /
    (bagGrams * 10)`, rounded to the nearest cent (half a cent rounds up).
  - `cupsText(cups)`: 0 is "Less than a cup left", 1 "About 1 cup left", else "About 8
    cups left".
  - `supplyLine(bean, supply, latestDoseTenths)`: `null` for a finished bag or no bag
    size. No dose: "340 g left" (grams left, rounded down, never below 0). Otherwise
    `cupsText`, plus " · $1.16 a cup" via `formatMoney` when the cost is given
    (including $0.00 for a bag that cost nothing).

## Testing

**Worked example** (from the outline): a 340 g bag at $22 with an 18 g dose costs about
$1.16 a cup; after ten 18 g brews, 160 g remain, about 8 cups left.

**Vitest (rules):**

- `src/coffee/beanSupply.test.ts`
  - `toTenths`: 18.1 is 181, 18 is 180, 18.5 is 185
  - `cupsLeft`: 340 g, 1800 used, dose 180 is 8 (the worked example); 340 g, ten 18.1 g
    brews (1810 used), dose 181 is 8 (159 g left); 340 g, 0 used, dose 180 is 18; 20 g
    bag, 180 used, dose 180 is 0 (2 g left); 36 g bag, 180 used, dose 180 is 1 (exactly
    one dose left); more used than the bag held gives 0, never negative
  - `costPerCupCents`: 2200 cents, 340 g, dose 180 is 116 (the worked example); 0 cents is
    0; half a cent rounds up (2250 cents, 1000 g, dose 180 is 41, from 40.5)
  - `cupsText`: 0, 1 and 8 give the three wordings ("cup" for 1, "cups" otherwise)
  - `doseFor`: the bag's own last dose wins over the latest of any bag; no row for the bag
    gives the latest of any bag; neither gives `null`
  - `supplyLine`:
    - worked example: "About 8 cups left · $1.16 a cup"
    - a new 340 g $22 bag with no brews of its own, latest dose of any bag 18 g: "About 18
      cups left · $1.16 a cup"
    - no cost: "About 8 cups left"; cost 0: "About 8 cups left · $0.00 a cup"
    - no bag size: `null`; finished bag: `null`
    - no dose anywhere: "340 g left"
    - more logged than the bag held: "Less than a cup left · $1.16 a cup"

## Notes

- **A database function for the dose sums**, rejected: the stub's idea of fetching the
  brews of bags in use (a bag never marked finished keeps all its brews, and past 1000
  rows the sum silently stops, showing more cups than he has), and a view of sums per bag
  (a view runs as its owner by default and would skip the `brews` lock unless made with
  `security_invoker`; one pattern across the site, 1.8's, is easier to check).
- **Tenths of a gram from the database**, rejected: grams as decimals summed in the site.
  `dose_grams` holds one decimal, so times 10 is exact in the database; whole numbers
  keep ten 18.1 g doses at exactly 181 tenths each in every sum.
- **Finished bags left out in the function** (joined to `beans`), rejected: passing the
  list of bag ids from the site (longer request, and the database already knows).
- **`security invoker` plus `user_id = auth.uid()`, `search_path = ''` and the revoke**:
  as 1.8 explains; the join matches `user_id` as well, belt and braces.
- **Fallback dose from 1.7's `getLatestBrew()`**, rejected: a second column in the
  function that repeats on every row, or a new function. One existing row-fetch does it.
- **A separate load for the sums**, rejected: one load for list and sums (a failed sum
  would hide the bags, and **Use** with them).
- **Cost per cup rounded to the nearest cent**, rejected: rounding down (would show $1.16
  for $1.169). Cost 0 shows "$0.00 a cup": accurate, and leaving it out would read the
  same as "no cost typed".
- **The line on its own row line**, rejected: on the roast-age line ("Roasted 9 days ago
  · About 8 cups left · $1.16 a cup" wraps at 375px).
- **"Less than a cup left" when over-used**, rejected: "0 g left" or a negative amount (a
  wrong bag size or a weighed-out extra scoop shouldn't look like an error).
- **Not in this plan:** warnings when a bag runs low, cups left on a bean's own page, on
  finished bags, or on the brew screen's bag picker.
- **For later plans:** `bean_supply()` gives grams used per bag in use; anything that
  needs grams left (a low-bag warning) reuses it and `cupsLeft`. Next migration is
  `009-…`.
