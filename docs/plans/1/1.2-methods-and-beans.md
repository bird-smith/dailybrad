# Plan: Brewing methods and beans

**Status:** building: code done 2026-11-18, not pushed; waiting on Brad to run migration 002 (see "Build progress" at the end)
**Outline:** `docs/outlines/coffee-tracker.md`, sections "The first version" (Brewing methods, Beans, No connection), "How it works"
**Depends on:** `1.1-look-and-sign-in.md` (must be built first: sign-in, colours, routes, shared database client)

## Goal

Let him tell the site what he brews with and what he brews. He adds his brewing methods
(Hario Switch, V60 and so on) once, and adds each bag of beans as it arrives: name,
roaster, roast date, bag size, cost and, if he wants, region, varietal and tasting notes.
One bag is the **active bean**: the home screen shows it with how many days ago it was
roasted, and later plans pick it by default. When a bag runs out he marks it finished and
it leaves the list of bags in use. Everything saves to the online database, so his phone
and computer show the same list. If saving fails (no internet in the kitchen), he sees one
plain sentence and nothing he typed is lost. That way of handling failures is shared, and
every later screen that saves reuses it.

## Decisions (settled)

Answered 2026-11-18: every recommendation below was approved, and the plan follows it.

1. **Where does the list of methods live?** Recommended: a quiet **Brewing methods** link
   at the bottom of the Beans screen, so the home screen keeps exactly the three links the
   outline asks for. Methods are set up once and rarely touched. The alternative is a
   fourth home link (**Beans · Methods · Recipes · History**). (Steps 6 and 8.)
2. **Which bean details must he fill in?** Recommended: only the name. Everything else is
   optional, so adding a bag in a hurry is quick. Without a roast date the home screen
   can't show days since roast, and without bag size and cost the later "cups left" and
   "cost per cup" stay blank. (Step 3.)
3. **Can he delete a bean?** Recommended: yes, a small **Delete bean** at the bottom of a
   bean's page, with an "Are you sure?" step, for bags added by mistake. A bag he has
   drunk is **marked finished**, not deleted. Once brews exist (plan 1.5), that plan
   decides what happens to a bean that has brews. (Step 7.)
4. **The first bag becomes active by itself.** Recommended: when no bag is active, a newly
   added bag becomes the active one, so he doesn't need an extra tap. Later bags don't.
   (Step 7.)
5. **Money sign.** Recommended: costs show with **$** as in the outline. If he pays in
   another currency, it is a one-line change. (Step 3.)

## What he sees

**Home screen (changed from 1.1):** between the date and the **Brew** button, a quiet
card shows the active bean:

- The bean's name in the serif, the roaster underneath in muted text, and "Roasted 9 days
  ago" (or "Roasted today", "Roasted yesterday"). No roast date: that line is left out.
- Tapping the card opens the Beans screen.
- No active bean: the card says "No bean chosen" and **Choose a bean**. No beans at all:
  **Add your first bean**. Both lead to the Beans screen.
- While loading, the card's space stays empty (no flicker or spinner). If loading fails:
  "Can't load your bean right now." with a **Try again** link.

**Beans screen (`#/coffee/beans`, replacing "not built yet"):**

- Heading **Beans**, a **Back** link, and a large **Add bean** button (the main action).
- The bags in use, one row each: name, roaster, roast age. The active bag is first and
  marked **Active** in copper. Every other row has a **Use** button on the right that
  makes it the active bag straight away.
- Tapping a row's name opens that bean's page.
- Below, a collapsed **Finished bags (4)** line; tapping it shows them, greyed, each
  opening its page. Hidden entirely when there are none.
- At the bottom, the small **Brewing methods** link (decision 1).

**A bean's page (`#/coffee/beans/new` to add, `#/coffee/beans/<id>` to edit):** one form,
one field per line, labels above the boxes:

Name, Roaster, Roast date (the phone's date picker; can't be later than today), Bag size
(grams), Cost ($), Region, Varietal, Notes (a taller box). Number boxes bring up the
phone's number keypad. A large **Save** button at the bottom returns to the Beans list.
Problems show one plain line under the box concerned, for example "Bag size should be a
whole number of grams." On an existing bean, below Save, two quiet links:
**Finished this bag** (or **Back in use** on a finished bag) and **Delete bean**, which
asks "Delete this bean? This can't be undone." with **Delete** and **Cancel**.

**Brewing methods screen (`#/coffee/methods`):**

- Heading **Brewing methods**, a **Back** link, one box "Add a method" with an **Add**
  button next to it (Enter also adds).
- While the list is empty, four one-tap suggestions underneath: **Hario Switch**,
  **V60**, **OXO Rapid Brewer**, **Cafelat Robot**. Each adds that method and disappears.
- The methods as a plain list. Tapping a name turns it into a box to rename it, with
  **Save** and **Cancel**. Each row has a quiet **Remove**, which asks "Remove V60?"
  inline with **Remove** and **Cancel**.
- Adding a name he already has: "You already have V60."

**When saving fails (every save on these screens):** the button goes back from
"Saving…" to its usual word, everything typed stays in the boxes, and one sentence shows
above the button:

- No internet or the database is asleep: "Couldn't save: can't reach the internet. What
  you typed is still here. Try again when you're connected."
- Signed out in the meantime: "Couldn't save: you've been signed out. Sign in again in a
  new tab, then try again." (A new tab, so this screen and what he typed stay put.)
- Anything else: "Couldn't save. Try again in a minute." Never the raw technical text.

Lists that fail to load show "Can't load your beans right now." (or methods) and **Try
again**.

**Desktop:** the same screens in the calm centred column from 1.1. Nothing here needs
side by side.

**Phone and computer:** each screen fetches fresh data when it opens. A bean added on the
phone appears on the computer the next time that screen is opened or the page reloaded.

## Data

All in the online database (Supabase), in two new tables made and locked by migration
`002`. Only he, signed in, can read or change them; signed-out visitors get nothing.

- **methods:** name (1 to 40 characters, no two the same ignoring capitals), date added.
- **beans:** name (required, up to 100 characters), roaster, roast date, bag size in
  whole grams (1 to 5000), cost in cents (0 to $1000), region, varietal, notes (up to
  2000 characters), finished yes/no, active yes/no, date added.
- **The database itself guarantees** at most one active bag, and that a finished bag is
  never active.
- Each row carries his user number (`user_id`), filled in by the database, never by the
  site. Personal entries live only in the database, never in a repo file.
- Nothing is kept in browser storage, so the Export/Import rule doesn't apply here;
  backups of these tables come in `1.9-backups.md`.

Write `supabase/migrations/002-methods-and-beans.sql`:

```sql
create table methods (
  id uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  user_id uuid not null default auth.uid() references auth.users on delete cascade,
  name text not null check (char_length(name) between 1 and 40),
  created_at timestamptz not null default now()
);
create unique index methods_name_once on methods (user_id, lower(name));
alter table methods enable row level security;
create policy "own methods only" on methods
  for all to authenticated
  using (user_id = auth.uid())
  with check (user_id = auth.uid());

create table beans (
  id uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  user_id uuid not null default auth.uid() references auth.users on delete cascade,
  name text not null check (char_length(name) between 1 and 100),
  roaster text check (char_length(roaster) <= 100),
  roast_date date,
  bag_grams integer check (bag_grams between 1 and 5000),
  cost_cents integer check (cost_cents between 0 and 100000),
  region text check (char_length(region) <= 100),
  varietal text check (char_length(varietal) <= 100),
  notes text check (char_length(notes) <= 2000),
  finished boolean not null default false,
  is_active boolean not null default false,
  created_at timestamptz not null default now(),
  check (not (finished and is_active))
);
create unique index beans_one_active on beans (user_id) where is_active;
alter table beans enable row level security;
create policy "own beans only" on beans
  for all to authenticated
  using (user_id = auth.uid())
  with check (user_id = auth.uid());

grant select, insert, update, delete on methods, beans to authenticated;

-- Switch the active bag in one go, so there is never a moment with two.
create function set_active_bean(bean_id uuid) returns void
language sql security invoker set search_path = ''
as $$
  update public.beans set is_active = false where is_active and id <> bean_id;
  update public.beans set is_active = true where id = bean_id and not finished;
$$;
revoke execute on function set_active_bean(uuid) from public, anon;
grant execute on function set_active_bean(uuid) to authenticated;
```

### 3. Rules (pure functions, tested)

- `src/coffee/types.ts`: `Method` and `Bean` types matching the tables (hand-written).
- `src/coffee/methodName.ts`: `checkMethodName(typed, existingNames, ownName?)` trims,
  rejects empty, over 40 characters, or a name already in the list ignoring capitals
  (except its own name when renaming). Returns the clean name or one plain sentence.
- `src/coffee/beanForm.ts`:
  - `parseMoney(text)`: accepts "22", "22.5", "22.50", "$22.50", spaces around; returns
    cents (2250) or "not a price". Blank is no cost.
  - `formatMoney(cents)`: 2250 to "22.50" for the box; with `$` for display.
  - `checkBean(form, today)`: trims every text box, turns blanks into "not given",
    requires the name (decision 2), checks lengths as in the table, bag size a whole
    number 1 to 5000, cost via `parseMoney` up to $1000, roast date not after today.
    Returns either the clean values ready to save or a message per box.
  - `beanToForm(bean)`: the reverse, to fill the form when editing.
- `src/coffee/roastAge.ts`: `daysSinceRoast(roastDate, today)` counts calendar days in
  local time (safe across clock changes); `roastAgeLabel(days)` gives "Roasted today",
  "Roasted yesterday", "Roasted 9 days ago", or nothing when there's no roast date.
- `src/coffee/beanList.ts`: `sortBeans(beans)` puts the active bag first, then newest
  roast date, bags without a roast date after those, then by name; `splitFinished`
  separates bags in use from finished ones.

## Notes

- **Active bag as a yes/no on each bean, with a database guarantee of one**, chosen over
  a separate one-row "settings" table: no extra table to lock and no empty row to create
  on first use. The `set_active_bean` function switches it in one step; two separate
  updates from the site could leave two active bags or none if the connection dropped
  between them.
- **Grants line in 002:** Supabase normally lets signed-in users reach new tables, but it
  has been changing that default. Stating it makes the file work either way; the policies
  still limit him to his own rows.
- **Cost in cents, not decimals:** whole numbers avoid rounding slips in the later
  cost-per-cup sum (`1.10-bean-supply.md`).
- **Hand-written types**, rejected Supabase's type generator: it needs the Supabase
  command-line tool and an access token, too much for two small tables.
- **No data library** (such as TanStack Query): a small `useLoad` hook is enough for
  screens that load once on opening. Revisit if many screens need the same data at once.
- **No offline queue:** keeping unsaved brews in the browser to send later would add
  browser storage, which needs Export/Import, and tricky clashes between devices. The
  outline only asks that what he typed stays on screen so he can try again.
- **No live updates between devices:** fresh data on opening a screen is enough for one
  person; live syncing would be built ahead of need.
- **Method suggestions** come from the outline's own examples and save typing on a
  phone; they vanish once he has any method.
- **Rejected: bean details on the list rows** (bag size, cost, notes): kept on the bean's
  page to keep the list calm. Cups left and cost per cup arrive in 1.10.
- **For later plans:**
  - Reuse `dataMessage`, `ErrorLine` and `useLoad` for every save and load; don't write a
    second error helper.
  - `getActiveBean()` in `src/coffee/beansData.ts` gives the default bean for brewing
    (1.4); the picker there lists only bags not finished.
  - Recipes (1.3) and brews (1.5) will point at `methods.id`, and brews at `beans.id`.
    Removing a method or deleting a bean that's in use must then not silently break
    them: recommended `on delete restrict` plus a plain "This method has recipes" message;
    those plans decide and update the Remove and Delete buttons here.
  - Cups left and cost per cup use `beans.bag_grams` and `beans.cost_cents`; either may
    be empty.
  - Next migration is `003-…`.
