# Plan: Repeat and recommended brews

**Status:** improved, not built
**Outline:** `docs/outlines/coffee-tracker.md`, sections "The first version" (Choosing a brew, Recommendations)
**Depends on:** `1.7-ratings.md` (must be built first: rating columns on `brews`,
`RatingDots.tsx`, `useLoad` with its list of things to watch), and through it 1.5
(`brewsData.ts`, `draftFromBrew`, `brewDayLabel`), 1.4 (Choose a brew, the brew screen,
`BrewDraft`, `draftFromRecipe`), 1.3, 1.2 and 1.1. `1.6-brew-timer.md` should be built
first too: step 6 checks the timer on a repeat brew (skip that one check if it isn't).

## Goal

Make Choose a brew answer "what should I make?". Once he taps a method, a **Repeat** card
at the top offers his latest brew with that method: tap it and the brew screen opens with
the same recipe, dose, grind and other settings he used last time. Below it, that
method's recipes are ranked by their average rating across all beans, each showing how
many rated brews the average comes from ("4.3 from 6 brews"), so one lucky 5 doesn't look
like a sure thing. The top one is marked **Recommended**. Once the bag he has chosen has
a few rated brews with a recipe, that bag's own average shows beside the overall one.
Unrated brews never count, and recipes with no ratings yet are still listed and can be
tapped as before.

## Decisions (settled)

Answered 2026-11-18: every recommendation below was approved, and the plan follows it.

1. **Which bag does Repeat use?** Recommended: **the bag chosen on Choose a brew** (his
   active bag unless he switched), because last brew's bag may be finished and repeating
   usually means "the same recipe with what's in the grinder today". The alternative:
   the bag the last brew used (an exact repeat, but it would happily brew from a bag he
   finished yesterday). (Step 5.)
2. **How many rated brews before a bag's own average shows?** Recommended: **3**. Below
   that it is mostly noise. The alternative is 2 (shows sooner, noisier) or 5 (steadier,
   but a 250 g bag may be gone first). (Step 3, `BEAN_AVERAGE_MIN`.)
3. **Can a recipe with one rated brew be "Recommended"?** Recommended: **yes, rank by the
   plain average** and rely on "from 1 brew" showing beside it, as the outline says. The
   alternative: a recipe needs at least 3 rated brews before it can rank above recipes
   that have them (safer, but a new recipe that tastes great stays low for a while, and
   the ranking is harder to explain). (Step 3, `rankRecipes`.)
4. **What exactly does Repeat copy?** Recommended: **everything as he brewed it**: dose,
   ratio, grinder, grind size, temperature, bloom, and the pours and steps of that day,
   even if the recipe has been edited since. That is what "repeat" means, and
   **Use recipe settings** still takes him back to the recipe as it is now. The
   alternative: dose and grind from the last brew, everything else from today's recipe.
   (Step 3, `startingDraft`.)

## What he sees

**Choose a brew (`#/coffee/brew`), after he taps a method.** The bean row and method
buttons from 1.4 stay as they are. Below them, in this order:

- **Repeat** card, shown only when he has brewed this method before (with a recipe that
  still belongs to it). One tappable card, at least 56px tall, with a thin border: a small
  muted "Repeat last brew" label, the recipe name in the serif, then a muted line of what
  he used, "18.5 g · 22 clicks · Yesterday · 7:42 AM" (grind left out when blank), and the
  brew's small rating dots when it was rated. Tapping it opens the brew screen set up as
  that brew. With no bag chosen it is greyed and not tappable, like the recipe rows.
- **Recipes**, ranked best first instead of A to Z. Each row keeps 1.4's recipe name and
  muted numbers ("1:15 · 18 g · 3 pours") and adds a third muted line:
  - rated: "4.3 from 6 brews", and when the chosen bag has 3 or more rated brews with
    this recipe, "· this bag 4.5 from 3";
  - not rated yet: "No ratings yet". These come after the rated ones, A to Z.
  - The first row, when it is rated, has a small copper **Recommended** label above its
    name. Nothing is labelled when no recipe has a rating.
- While the ranking loads after a method tap: the 1.4 recipe area shows "Loading…" (a
  second or less). If it fails: the recipes A to Z as in 1.4, still tappable, with one
  muted line above them, "Can't load ratings right now." and **Try again**. Brewing is
  never blocked by the ranking.
- Switching bag: the "this bag" parts update; the order doesn't change (it's ranked on
  all beans).

**The brew screen opened from Repeat (`#/coffee/brew/<recipe>/<bean>/<brew>`):**

- As in 1.4, with a muted line under the heading's line: "Repeating your last brew ·
  Yesterday · 7:42 AM". The dose, today's settings, pours and steps are that brew's; the
  "Recipe: …" lines under **Change for today** show where it differs from the recipe.
- **Use recipe settings** goes back to the recipe as it is now (dose included).
- The timer (1.6) and **Save brew** work as on any brew screen; saving logs a new brew.
- If that brew has been deleted, or no longer belongs to this recipe: the screen starts
  from the recipe with the muted line "That brew isn't here any more, so this starts from
  the recipe."

**Desktop:** the same, in the centred column from 1.1.

## Data

- **Nothing new is saved and no table changes.** Migration `006-recipe-ratings.sql` adds
  one read-only database function, `recipe_ratings`, that adds up his ratings for one
  method: per recipe, how many rated brews and their total, overall and for one bag.
- It runs **as the person asking** (`security invoker`), so the `brews` lock from 004
  applies: it only ever sees his own brews. Signed-out visitors may not run it at all.
- Why a function: averages over every brew would otherwise mean downloading every brew,
  and Supabase hands over at most 1000 rows per request. The function returns one row per
  recipe that has ratings for that method, so a handful of rows, however many brews.
- Only brews with a rating count; taste words and notes are ignored. Brews whose recipe
  was deleted (`recipe_id` empty) count for no recipe. A brew counts for the method it
  was **brewed** with (`brews.method_id`), even if its recipe was moved since.
- The Repeat card asks for one brew: the newest with this method and a recipe link.
- Nothing in browser storage, so no Export/Import here (1.9 backs up `brews`; the
  function holds no data of its own).

### 2. Migration 006 and hand batch A

Write `supabase/migrations/006-recipe-ratings.sql`:

```sql
-- Rating sums per recipe for one method: overall, and for one bag.
-- Runs as the caller, so the "own brews only" lock on brews applies.
create function recipe_ratings(p_method_id uuid, p_bean_id uuid)
returns table (
  recipe_id uuid,
  rated_brews integer,
  rating_total integer,
  bean_rated_brews integer,
  bean_rating_total integer
)
language sql stable security invoker
set search_path = ''
as $$
  select b.recipe_id,
         count(*)::integer,
         sum(b.rating)::integer,
         (count(*) filter (where b.bean_id = p_bean_id))::integer,
         coalesce(sum(b.rating) filter (where b.bean_id = p_bean_id), 0)::integer
  from public.brews b
  where b.user_id = auth.uid()
    and b.method_id = p_method_id
    and b.recipe_id is not null
    and b.rating is not null
  group by b.recipe_id;
$$;

-- New functions can be run by everyone unless told otherwise.
revoke execute on function recipe_ratings(uuid, uuid) from public, anon;
grant execute on function recipe_ratings(uuid, uuid) to authenticated;
```

New query**, paste the file, **Run**.

### 3. Rules (pure functions, tested)

- `src/coffee/types.ts` (add): `RecipeRating` matching the function's columns,
  hand-written.
- `src/coffee/recommendations.ts`:
  - `BEAN_AVERAGE_MIN = 3` (decision 2).
  - `averageOf(total, count)`: `null` when count is 0, otherwise the exact average.
  - `rankRecipes(recipes, ratings)`: `recipes` is the chosen method's list (from 1.3's
    `groupRecipes`, as Choose a brew already has it). Each row: the recipe, its average
    and rated-brew count, and the bag's average and count **only** when the bag count is
    at least `BEAN_AVERAGE_MIN`. Rating rows for recipes not in the list are ignored (a
    recipe moved to another method). Order: rated first by exact average (high to low),
    ties by more rated brews, then name A to Z; unrated after, A to Z. Decision 3:
    plain average.
  - `recommendedId(rows)`: the first row's recipe id when it is rated, else none.
  - `ratingLine(row)`: "4.3 from 6 brews", "5 from 1 brew", plus " · this bag 4.5 from 3"
    when there is a bag average; "No ratings yet" when unrated. Averages shown with 1.4's
    `formatOneDecimal`, after rounding to one decimal (4.25 shows "4.3", 4.0 shows "4").
  - `repeatChoice(brew, methodRecipes)`: the brew when its `recipe_id` is one of this
    method's recipes, otherwise none.
  - `repeatSummary(brew, now)`: "18.5 g · 22 clicks · Yesterday · 7:42 AM", using
    `formatOneDecimal` and `brewDayLabel`; grind size left out when blank.
  - `startingDraft(recipe, beanId, brew)`: with no brew, or a brew whose `recipe_id` isn't
    this recipe, `{ draft: draftFromRecipe(recipe, beanId), repeating: false }`.
    Otherwise `draftFromBrew(brew)` with the bean set to `beanId` (decision 1), the
    recipe id set to this recipe, and `totalSeconds` (the target time, which a brew
    doesn't store) taken from the recipe the way `draftFromRecipe` does, so the 1.6 timer
    still has it; `bloomSeconds` stays the brew's. `{ draft, repeating: true }`.
  - Field names (`totalSeconds`, `bloomSeconds`, the recipe's target time column) as 1.4
    and 1.6 actually built them.

## Notes

- **A database function for the sums**, rejected: downloading every rated brew and
  averaging in the site (stops silently at 1000 rows, and grows forever), paging through
  them all (many requests on every method tap), and a view (a view of sums per recipe
  and bag can't take "this method, this bag" as inputs, so it returns a row per recipe
  per bag per method, which grows with every bag and would hit 1000 again in time). The
  function returns sums and counts; dividing, rounding and ranking stay in TypeScript
  where Vitest checks them.
- **`security invoker` plus `user_id = auth.uid()`**: the lock on `brews` already limits
  it to his rows; the extra line keeps it right even if someone later changes how it
  runs. `set search_path = ''` with `public.` names is Supabase's own advice for
  functions. The revoke is needed because Postgres lets everyone run a new function.
- **Brew id in the path** (`…/<recipe>/<bean>/<brew>`), rejected: passing dose and grind
  in the address (only part of the settings, and a grind like "22 clicks" in an address
  is ugly) and a `?repeat=` search value (1.6 rewrites the search part for its timer
  and could drop it). A brew id also survives a refresh.
- **`startingDraft` on top of `draftFromBrew`**, rejected: giving `draftFromRecipe` more
  options for dose and grind. A repeat copies every setting, so the brew is the natural
  source; only the bag, recipe id and target time need filling in.
- **The latest brew per method with a recipe link** for the Repeat card: a brew whose
  recipe was deleted has no recipe screen to open. If the newest V60 brew lost its recipe,
  the card shows the newest one that still has one.
- **Ranked list with a label, not a separate Recommended card**, rejected: a card above
  the list that repeats the list's first row. One list, best first, keeps the screen
  calmer (one main action: pick something).
- **Rank on all beans, bag average beside it**, as the outline says; switching bag doesn't
  reshuffle the list under his thumb.
- **Ranking loads after the method tap**, rejected: loading every method's sums up
  front (more to load before anything shows). Showing the A-to-Z list on failure keeps
  brewing possible with a bad kitchen connection.
- **Rejected for now:** a "Brews like this" link from a ranked recipe (History's filter
  from 1.7 already does it from any brew), ranking by bag, charts, showing the average of
  taste words.
- **For later plans:**
  - 1.9: no new table; the function holds no data, so the backup file is unchanged. A
    restored backup's ratings show up in the ranking straight away.
  - 1.10: the same pattern (a `security invoker` function returning sums, `revoke` from
    `public, anon`, `grant` to `authenticated`) fits dose sums per bag without hitting
    the 1000-row limit.
  - Next migration is `007-…`.
