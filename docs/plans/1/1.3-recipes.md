**Outline:** `docs/outlines/coffee-tracker.md`, sections "The first version" (Recipes), "Rules and worked example"

## Goal

Let him save the recipes he knows, one method at a time: a name, the ratio, the coffee
dose, grinder and grind size, water temperature, bloom, total time and notes, plus an
optional list of pours and steps in order (such as "Close the valve" on the Hario Switch).
The water amount is worked out from dose and ratio as he types, so he never does the sum.
Pours are saved as shares of the total water, so a later plan can scale them to any dose.
A recipe for the Cafelat Robot can be just a dose, a ratio and a few steps. The Recipes
screen lists his recipes under each method; tapping one opens it to change or delete.
Everything saves to the online database, the same way beans do.

## Revision 2026-11-19: pours as fixed weights (built)

Brad's feedback after using it: "The math is off. Total water weighed was 240. I would like
to set the bloom by % of dose or a manual number. Same for other pours: fixed amount or %,
but generally fixed." Cause: every pour was saved as a share of the water (decision 1), so
brewing at another dose moved "pour to 100 g" to some other number. Answered with Noah:

- **Each pour is "pour to" a number with a g / % switch** beside the box, grams by default.
  % on the first pour (the bloom) is **of the dose** (250% of 18 g = 45 g); % on later
  pours is **of the water**. A % pour shows its grams at the recipe dose beside it. The
  last pour still reads "Pour to 270 g (the rest)" and always fills to the water.
- **Saved as typed:** `{ kind: 'pour', to, unit: 'g' | '%', at }`, `to` null on the last
  pour. Old `{ kind: 'pour', percent, at }` shares still read correctly
  (`pourTargets` in `recipeMath.ts`), because past brews and old backups hold them.
- **Migration 009** turned his saved recipes' shares into the fixed grams they showed at
  the recipe dose. Brews keep their shares (past brews stay as brewed).
- **Brew screen:** fixed pours stay put when the dose changes; only the last pour and %
  pours move. If the pours no longer fit the water (a much smaller dose), a plain line
  under Water says so.
- Recipes were already tied to a brewing method (method is required, list grouped by
  method). Check with Brad what he meant: maybe the brew screen should start from the
  method, which it already does.

Decision 1 and the "share" wording below are superseded by this section.

## Decisions (settled)

Answered 2026-11-18: every recommendation below was approved, and the plan follows it.

1. **How does he type pours?** Recommended: as "pour to" weights in grams at the recipe's
   own dose, the way recipes are written and the way he'll read the scale ("pour to 135
   g"). The last pour is always "to the end" (the full water), filled in for him. The site
   turns them into shares of the water when saving. The alternative is typing percentages
   (10%, 40%, 50%), which is simpler to build but means doing sums by hand. (Steps 3, 7.)
2. **What must a recipe have?** Recommended: method, name, ratio and dose. Everything else
   is optional, so a half-known recipe can be saved and filled in later. Without a dose
   the site can't show the water or turn "pour to" weights into shares. (Steps 3, 7.)
3. **Celsius or Fahrenheit?** Recommended: **°C**, as most pour-over recipes are written,
   and the scale already works in grams. If his kettle shows °F, it is a one-line change.
   (Step 3.)
4. **Times on steps.** Recommended: each pour or step can have an optional "at" time from
   the start (pour to 135 g **at 0:45**, open the valve **at 2:00**), so the later timer
   (1.6) knows when each step comes. Without them the timer can only count up. (Step 7.)
5. **Removing a method that has recipes.** Recommended: the site refuses with "V60 has
   recipes. Delete them or move them to another method first." The alternative, deleting
   the method's recipes along with it, loses work with one tap. (Step 4.)

## Data

All in the online database (Supabase), in one new table made and locked by migration
`003`. Only he, signed in, can read or change it.

- **recipes:** which method (required), name (1 to 60 characters, no two the same within
  one method, ignoring capitals), ratio (1 to 30, one decimal place), dose in grams (1 to
  100, one decimal place), grinder, grind size, temperature (whole degrees), bloom and
  total time (whole seconds), notes (up to 2000 characters), pours and steps, date added.
- **Pours and steps** are saved together inside the recipe as one ordered list. Each item
  is either a pour with its **share of the total water** (10, 40, 50: the outline's rule)
  or a step with its text; each may have an "at" time in seconds. Shares always add up to
  exactly 100.
- **Water is never saved:** it is always dose times ratio, so it can't disagree.
- A method can't be removed while recipes point at it (database rule).
- Each row carries his user number (`user_id`), filled in by the database. Nothing goes in
  browser storage, so no Export/Import here; backups come in `1.9-backups.md`.

Write `supabase/migrations/003-recipes.sql`:

```sql
-- Lets a recipe point at a method AND the same owner in one rule.
alter table methods add constraint methods_id_owner unique (id, user_id);

create table recipes (
  id uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  user_id uuid not null default auth.uid() references auth.users on delete cascade,
  method_id uuid not null,
  name text not null check (char_length(name) between 1 and 60),
  ratio numeric(3,1) not null check (ratio between 1 and 30),
  dose_grams numeric(4,1) not null check (dose_grams between 1 and 100),
  grinder text check (char_length(grinder) <= 60),
  grind_size text check (char_length(grind_size) <= 30),
  temperature integer check (temperature between 40 and 212),
  bloom_seconds integer check (bloom_seconds between 1 and 600),
  total_seconds integer check (total_seconds between 1 and 3600),
  steps jsonb not null default '[]'::jsonb
    check (jsonb_typeof(steps) = 'array' and jsonb_array_length(steps) <= 20),
  notes text check (char_length(notes) <= 2000),
  created_at timestamptz not null default now(),
  -- "no action": a method with recipes can't be removed (see Notes).
  foreign key (method_id, user_id) references methods (id, user_id) on delete no action
);
create unique index recipes_name_once on recipes (user_id, method_id, lower(name));
create index recipes_by_method on recipes (method_id, user_id);
alter table recipes enable row level security;
create policy "own recipes only" on recipes
  for all to authenticated
  using (user_id = auth.uid())
  with check (user_id = auth.uid());

grant select, insert, update, delete on recipes to authenticated;
```

### 3. Rules (pure functions, tested)

- `src/coffee/types.ts` (add to 1.2's file): `Recipe` matching the table, and
  `RecipeStep` = `{ kind: 'pour'; percent: number; at: number | null }` or
  `{ kind: 'action'; text: string; at: number | null }`. Hand-written. Note that
  `numeric` columns arrive as numbers.
- `src/coffee/recipeMath.ts` (also used by 1.4):
  - `totalWater(dose, ratio)`: dose times ratio, exact (18 and 15 give 270).
  - `pourTargets(percents, water)`: for each pour, the running total to pour to and the
    single amount. Running totals are rounded to whole grams, the last is exactly the
    rounded water, and single amounts are the differences, so they always add up.
  - `poursToPercents(pourTo, water)`: the reverse, for saving. Takes the "pour to"
    weights of all pours but the last; each share is rounded to two decimals and the last
    share is 100 minus the others, so the total is exactly 100.
- `src/coffee/clock.ts`: `parseClock(text)` reads "45", "045", "300", "3:00" and "0:45"
  (last two digits are seconds, the rest minutes); blank is "not given"; seconds over 59,
  letters, or over 60 minutes are refused with a sentence. `formatClock(seconds)` gives
  "0:45", "3:00".
- `src/coffee/recipeForm.ts`:
  - `TEMP_UNIT = '°C'` and its range, 70 to 100 (for °F: `'°F'`, 158 to 212),
    per decision 3.
  - `checkRecipe(form, otherNamesInMethod)`: trims every text box and turns blanks into
    "not given"; requires method, name, ratio and dose (decision 2); ratio and dose
    numbers with at most one decimal in range; name not already used in that method
    ignoring capitals; temperature a whole number in range; times via `parseClock`;
    every pour except the last a whole or one-decimal number, each more than the one
    before and less than the water; step text 1 to 60 characters; "at" times never going
    backwards down the list; at most 20 rows. Returns either the clean values ready to
    save (pours converted with `poursToPercents`) or a message per box or row.
  - `recipeToForm(recipe)`: the reverse, turning shares back into "pour to" weights at
    the recipe's dose with `pourTargets`.
- `src/coffee/recipeList.ts`: `groupRecipes(methods, recipes)` gives each method (in the
  given order) with its recipes A to Z ignoring capitals; `recipeSummary(recipe)` gives
  "1:15 · 18 g · 3 pours" ("1 pour", none left out; "15.5" kept, "15.0" shown as "15").

## Notes

- **Pours and steps in one list inside the recipe** (a `jsonb` column), rejected: a
  separate steps table. The list is always read and saved whole, so one row saves in one
  go with nothing half-written if the connection drops, and there's no second table to
  lock. The cost: the database checks only that it is a list of at most 20; the shape of
  each item is checked by `checkRecipe`. Fine for one person writing his own data.
- **One ordered list for pours and steps**, rejected: separate pour and step lists. The
  timer (1.6) needs to know a valve opens between the second and third pour.
- **Shares saved, "pour to" weights typed** (decision 1): the outline's rule is
  shares of the water; weights at the recipe dose are what he reads in recipes. The last
  pour is always "the rest", so shares can't fail to add up to 100.
- **Water not saved:** dose times ratio, so a changed dose can never leave a stale water
  amount.
- **Numbers as one-decimal `numeric`**, rejected: tenths stored as whole numbers (as 1.2
  does for cents). Ratios and doses are never added up into money, and 15.5 reads plainly
  in the table for later plans.
- **Temperature check 40 to 212 in the database** covers both °C and °F, so switching
  units (decision 3) needs no migration; the site applies the real range.
- **Times typed microwave-style** ("300" for 3:00): phone number keypads have no colon
  key. A colon typed on a computer works too. Rejected: separate minute and second boxes
  (twice the tapping).
- **Bloom kept as its own time**, as the outline lists it, alongside the optional "at"
  times. For 1.6: recommended that the timer uses "at" times when given, and otherwise
  waits the bloom time after the first pour.
- **`on delete no action`, not `restrict`** (1.2 suggested `restrict`): both refuse
  removing a method that has recipes, with code `23503`. `restrict` would also block
  deleting his whole account, because it fires before the account's own clean-up
  removes the recipes. 1.5 should do the same for brews pointing at beans and methods.
- **Method and owner checked together** (`methods_id_owner` and the two-column link):
  stops a recipe ever pointing at someone else's method, which a plain link to
  `methods.id` would allow, since links skip the row locks. Sign-ups are off so it can't
  happen today; it costs one line.
- **Rejected for now:** a "Make a copy" button for variations (not asked for; easy to add
  once he uses recipes), live water preview per pour on the list, and method-specific
  forms (the four step fill-ins cover the Switch without special cases).
- **For later plans:**
  - `src/coffee/recipeMath.ts` (`totalWater`, `pourTargets`) is the scaling rule for
    1.4; don't write a second one. 1.4 shows running totals large and single amounts
    small, both from `pourTargets`.
  - A recipe's `steps` items: `{ kind: 'pour', percent, at }` or `{ kind: 'action',
    text, at }`, `at` in seconds or `null`. 1.6 builds the timer from these, plus
    `bloom_seconds` and `total_seconds`.
  - `dose_grams` is the recipe's default dose for 1.4. Brews (1.5) copy settings from
    the recipe rather than pointing at its numbers, so editing a recipe never rewrites
    past brews. 1.5 decides what deleting a recipe that has brews does, and updates
    **Delete recipe** here.
  - `isInUseError` in `src/lib/dataMessage.ts` is the shared check for "other rows point
    at this"; 1.5 uses it for **Delete bean**.
  - `parseClock` / `formatClock` in `src/coffee/clock.ts` for any time box (1.5's brew
    time).
  - Next migration is `004-…`.
