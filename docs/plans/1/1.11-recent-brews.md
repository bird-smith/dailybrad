# Plan: Last three brews on the home screen

**Status:** built 2026-11-24.
**Outline:** `docs/outlines/coffee-tracker.md`, section "The first version" (Home screen)
**Depends on:** 1.5's brews and History screen (`#/coffee/history/<id>`), 1.7's
`RatingDots` and "How was it?" card.

## Goal

The home screen shows his last three brews, so he can see at a glance what he made and
how it went, and tap one to see everything about it. The links to the other screens sit
together at the bottom, under the Brew button.

## Decisions

Asked for on 2026-11-24 (Noah, passing on the request). The two points below were not in
the request and follow the recommendation; Brad can overturn either.

1. **The "How was it?" card stays** above the three brews when the newest brew has no
   rating, so rating is still one tap. The alternative, dropping the card and rating from
   the brew's own page, costs two extra taps every morning.
2. **An unrated brew** in the list shows a small "Not rated" instead of the dots.

## What he sees

Top to bottom, on phone and desktop alike:

1. "Coffee", today's date and the active bean card, as before.
2. The "How was it?" card, only while the newest brew has no rating.
3. **Recent brews:** up to three rows, newest first. Each row: the recipe name large, the
   rating dots (or "Not rated") on the right, and underneath, small and muted, the method
   and the day ("V60 · Today · 7:42 AM"). Tapping a row opens that brew in History; Back
   returns home. After rating in the card, the row's dots fill in straight away.
4. The big **Brew** button ("Brew saved." under it after saving a brew).
5. One row of links: **Methods · Recipes · Beans · History**.
6. **Backup · Sign out**, small, at the very bottom, as before.

With no brews yet, or if brews can't load, the Recent brews part doesn't show at all (the
bean card already says when the site can't load).

## Steps

1. `brewsData.ts`: `listRecentBrews()`, the newest 3 brews (`RECENT_BREWS = 3`).
2. New `src/coffee/RecentBrews.tsx`: loads those brews with the methods and recipes, and
   names them with 1.5's tested `historyRows`; each row links to `/coffee/history/<id>`.
3. `RateMeCard.tsx`: optional `onRated`, called after a rating saves.
4. `CoffeeHome.tsx`: the new order above; the card's `onRated` reloads the list; the
   Methods link joins the bottom links.

No database change. No new rule or sum, so no new test (row naming is already tested).

## Check by eye

- [ ] Phone width: three rows, Brew button, four links on one line, nothing cramped.
- [ ] Tap a row: the brew's details open; Back returns home.
- [ ] Rate the newest brew in the card: its row's dots fill in.
- [ ] A new account with no brews: no Recent brews heading, no gap.
