# Plan: Rating brews

**Status:** built 2026-11-19. Migration 006 run with `npm run migrate`; signed-out read of
`brews` refused (401, checked 2026-11-19). `useLoad` already re-ran on a watch list and
ignored late answers, so it was left unchanged. Live-site checks by hand (1-8) are his to do.
**Outline:** `docs/outlines/coffee-tracker.md`, sections "The first version" (Rating, Home screen), "How it works"
**Depends on:** `1.5-brew-log.md` (must be built first: `brews` table, `brewsData.ts`,
`brewLog.ts`, `HistoryScreen.tsx`, `BrewDetails.tsx`, "Brew saved." on home), and through

still lands on home with "Brew saved."

## Goal

After tasting, he gives each brew 1 to 5 dots, can tap a few taste words ("sour",
"bitter", "weak", "strong", "balanced") and can add a short note. The home screen offers a
"rate me" card whenever his newest brew has no rating yet, so rating takes two taps in the
kitchen. Every brew's page in History shows its rating and lets him rate it or change it.
History can be narrowed to one bean, method and recipe, so he can see how that
combination has been going. Averages and ranking are `1.8-recommendations.md`.

## Decisions (settled)

Answered 2026-11-18: every recommendation below was approved, and the plan follows it.

1. **Rating from the home card.** Recommended: the five dots are **in the card itself**;
   tapping one opens the taste words, the note and **Save rating** right there. The
   alternative, a card that opens the brew's page in History, is one more screen and a
   Back every morning. (Steps 5, 6.)
2. **A brew he doesn't want to rate** (spilled, a test). Recommended: **no skip button**.
   The card only ever asks about the newest brew, so it goes away by itself with the next
   brew; deleting the brew also removes it. The alternative, a **Not this one** link, needs
   an extra "skipped" mark on every brew and is one more thing on the home screen. (Step 6.)
3. **Words and note without dots.** Recommended: **dots first**: the words, note and
   **Save rating** appear only once a dot is tapped. The alternative lets him save only
   words or a note, but then the brew still counts as unrated, so the card keeps asking.
   (Steps 2, 3, 5.)
4. **How he picks brews to compare.** Recommended: **both** a **Brews like this** link on
   every brew's page (one tap: same bean, method and recipe) and a quiet **Filter** on
   History with three choices (bean, method, recipe) for "every brew of this bag". The
   alternative, only the link, is less to build but can't show a bean across all its
   recipes. (Steps 7, 8.)

## What he sees

**The dots.** Five plain round dots in a row. Chosen ones are filled copper; the rest are
thin outlined circles. When he is rating, each dot sits in a 48px tap area; tapping the
third fills three. Elsewhere (History rows) they are small and only show.

**The rating form** (the same on home and on a brew's page):

- The five dots. Nothing else shows until a dot is tapped (decision 1).
- Then: five taste-word buttons in a wrapping row, each at least 44px tall. Tapping one
  marks it (copper outline and text); tapping again unmarks it. Any mix is allowed.
- **Note (optional)**, a three-line box, 16px text, at most 300 characters.
- **Save rating**, an outlined ink button 48px tall (copper stays for **Brew**), "Saving…"
  while it works, not double-tappable. **Cancel** beside it only when changing a rating.
- Failure: the usual one sentence (`ErrorLine`), and the dots, words and note stay. The
  brew deleted meanwhile (another device): "This brew isn't here any more."

**Home (changed):** under the **Brew** button and the "Brew saved." line from 1.5, when
his newest brew has no rating, a quiet card:

- "How was it?" in the serif, then muted: "Kasuya · Today · 7:42 AM".
- The rating form. After saving, the card is replaced by a muted "Rating saved." that
  goes away when he leaves the screen.
- Newest brew rated, or no brews yet: no card, no empty space. While loading, nothing. If
  loading fails, nothing either (the active-bean card already says the site can't load).

Right after **Save brew**, home shows "Brew saved." with the card under it, ready for
when he has tasted.

**A brew's page in History (changed):** a **Rating** section between the date line and
**Bean**:

- Rated: the dots, the taste words in muted text ("sour · weak"), the note as written, and
  a quiet **Change rating** link that opens the form filled in, with **Cancel**.
- Not rated: "Not rated yet." and the dots ready to tap.
- At the bottom, beside **Fix this brew** and **Delete brew**, a quiet **Brews like this**
  link: History showing only brews with this bean, method and recipe (bean and method only
  if the recipe has been deleted).

**History list (changed):**

- Each row shows the small dots at the right end of its first line. Unrated rows show
  nothing there.
- A quiet **Filter** link above the list opens three dropdowns: **All beans** (bags in
  use, then finished bags), **All methods**, **All recipes** (each as "Kasuya · V60").
  With any chosen, a muted line "Showing: Ethiopia Guji · V60 · Kasuya" and **Clear**.
- Paging stays 50 at a time with **Show older brews**, now within the filter.
- Nothing matches: "No brews match." with **Clear**.
- The filter is part of the address, so Back, a refresh and tapping into a brew and back

**Desktop:** the same, in History's two columns from 1.5: Filter sits above the list
column; rating a brew in the right column updates its row's dots on the left at once.

## Data

All in the online database (Supabase), on the existing `brews` table. Only he, signed in,
can read or change it (1.5's lock already covers new columns).

- **rating:** 1 to 5, or empty for "not rated yet". Unrated brews are left out of
  averages (1.8).
- **taste_words:** a list drawn only from sour, bitter, weak, strong, balanced; empty by
  default.
- **rating_note:** up to 300 characters, or empty.
- The database refuses words or a note on a brew with no rating (decision 3), any other
  word, and a rating outside 1 to 5.
- Nothing goes in browser storage, so no Export/Import here; these columns ride along with
  `brews` in `1.9-backups.md`.

### 2. Migration 006 and hand batch A

Write `supabase/migrations/006-brew-ratings.sql`:

```sql
alter table brews
  add column rating smallint check (rating between 1 and 5),
  add column taste_words text[] not null default '{}'
    check (taste_words <@ array['sour','bitter','weak','strong','balanced']::text[]
           and cardinality(taste_words) <= 5),
  add column rating_note text check (char_length(rating_note) between 1 and 300),
  -- Words and a note only on a rated brew (decision 3).
  add constraint brews_rating_first
    check (rating is not null or (taste_words = '{}' and rating_note is null));
```

No new table, so no new lock: the "own brews only" policy and the grant from 004 cover
every column. Existing brews become unrated with no words.

Hand batch A (click-by-click, `.claude/skills/click-by-click/SKILL.md`): **SQL Editor →
New query**, paste the file, **Run**.

### 3. Rules (pure functions, tested)

    `TASTE_WORDS` order with no repeats.

    rating_note }` or the sentences.

  - `ratingLabel(rating)`: "Rated 4 out of 5" or "Not rated yet" (for screen readers and

  - `needsRating(brew)`: true only for a brew with no rating; false with no brew.

## Notes

- **Columns on `brews`, not a separate ratings table**: one rating per brew, always read
  with it. Rejected: a `ratings` table (a join for every History page and for 1.8, and a
  second thing to back up and lock).
- **Taste words as a `text[]` list with a fixed set checked by the database**, rejected:
  five yes/no columns (adding a word later means a new column each time, and five checks)
  and a separate words table (overbuilt for five fixed words). Adding a word later is a
  new migration that replaces the check, plus `TASTE_WORDS`.
- **"Dots first" rule in the database too** (`brews_rating_first`), so a half-finished
  rating can't sneak in from another device or old code and leave a brew "rated" in
  words but missing from averages.
- **Note limit 300**, rejected: none (a box that can grow without end on a phone) and the
  60 of names (too short for "grind finer next time, bitter finish").
- **No `rated_at` date**: nothing on the site needs it. Rejected as overbuilding.
- **Any mix of words**, rejected: blocking "weak" with "strong" (rules he didn't ask for;
  a brew can honestly be weak and bitter at once).
- **The card asks only about the newest brew** (the outline's "the last brew"). An older
  unrated brew is rated from History. Rejected: a list of every unrated brew (a to-do list
  on the home screen, against "nothing that isn't needed right now").
- **Card shows nothing while loading or on failure**: the active-bean card already tells
  him the site can't load; two such sentences on one calm screen is noise, and the brew
  waits in History either way.
- **Card uses the brew's saved recipe name**, not the recipe's current one (1.5's rule),
  to avoid loading every recipe for one line. Only a recipe renamed between brewing and
  rating shows its old name there; History stays correct.
- **`rateBrew` separate from `updateBrew`**, so rating never touches the settings and
  **Fix this brew** never touches the rating. The changed row is asked for back so a
  deleted brew is noticed rather than "saved" into nothing.
- **Filtering in the database query**, rejected: filtering the 50 rows already loaded
  (would miss older brews and look complete when it isn't).
- **Filter in the address** (`?bean=…&method=…&recipe=…`), rejected: screen memory only
  (lost on refresh, and **Brews like this** couldn't be a plain link).
- **Brews of a deleted recipe** match no recipe in the filter; they still show under "All
  recipes" and in a bean or method filter.
- **Rejected for now:** averages or counts on the filtered list (1.8's job), sorting by
  rating, charts (not in the first version), rating while the timer runs, a "skip" mark
  (decision 2).
- **For later plans:**
  - 1.8: average only rows with `rating` not null (`.not('rating', 'is', null)`); taste
    words and notes aren't part of ranking. Select only the columns it needs and stay
    under the 1000-row limit (for example, a database view or function that returns the
    averages, in its own migration, or paging). The History filter and `brewsLikeThis`
    are there if 1.8 wants a "see these brews" link from a ranked recipe.
  - 1.9: `rating`, `taste_words` (a JSON list) and `rating_note` are plain `brews` columns;
    on import, a brew with words or a note but no rating is refused by the database, so
    import must not drop the rating.
  - 1.10: nothing here; dose sums ignore ratings.
  - Next migration is `007-…`.
