# Plan: Saving brews and the history list

**Status:** improved, not built
**Outline:** `docs/outlines/coffee-tracker.md`, sections "The first version" (Brew logging, Beans), "Rules and worked example"
**Depends on:** `1.4-brew-setup.md` (must be built first: `brewSetup.ts`, `BrewScreen.tsx`),
and through it `1.3-recipes.md`, `1.2-methods-and-beans.md` and `1.1-look-and-sign-in.md`

## Goal

Make a brew count. At the bottom of the brew screen from 1.4 he types how long it took
(or leaves it blank) and taps **Save brew**. Everything about that cup is copied into
the log: the bag, the method, the recipe's name and today's settings (dose, ratio,
grinder, grind size, temperature, bloom, pours and steps). Editing or deleting the
recipe later never changes a past brew. **History** lists his brews, newest first; tapping
one shows everything it was made with, plus how many days after roasting it was. He can
fix a brew he logged wrong, or delete it. Ratings are 1.7, the timer 1.6, and cups left
and cost per cup on the Beans screen `1.10-bean-supply.md` (split out of this plan).

## Decisions (settled)

Answered 2026-11-18: every recommendation below was approved, and the plan follows it.

1. **Deleting a bean or method that has brews.** Recommended: the site **refuses**, with
   "This bean has brews, so it can't be deleted. Mark it finished instead." (and "V60 is
   used by recipes or brews, so it can't be removed."). The alternative deletes the brews
   along with it: one tap loses months of history. (Steps 2, 7.)
2. **Deleting a recipe that has brews.** Recommended: the recipe goes, its **brews stay**
   in History with the recipe's name and the settings they were made with. The
   alternative refuses, like methods; then a recipe he tried and disliked can never be
   tidied away without deleting its brews. (Steps 2, 7.)
3. **What "Fix this brew" can change.** Recommended: the bag, dose, ratio, grinder, grind
   size, temperature, bloom and brew time. **Not** the method, recipe, pours or steps (a
   different recipe is a different brew: delete it and log again) and not the date. The
   alternative, everything, makes the fix form a copy of the recipe form. (Step 6.)
4. **After saving.** Recommended: back to the **home screen** with a small "Brew saved."
   line, ready for the "rate me" card in 1.7. The alternative opens the saved brew in
   History (reassuring the first few times, an extra Back every day after). (Step 5.)

This plan also assumes 1.4's answer that **every brew has a bean**. If he chose
otherwise there, `bean_id` in step 2 loses `not null` and History shows "No bean".

## What he sees

**The brew screen (changed from 1.4):** the muted "Saving brews and the timer come next."
line is replaced by:

- **Brew time** (optional), typed microwave-style like the recipe page ("310" is 3:10),
  with the tidy time beside it. Blank by default.
- A large copper **Save brew** button (the main action), "Saving…" while it works.
- If any box on the screen shows a problem: "Fix the boxes marked above first." and
  nothing is saved. Saving fails: one plain sentence above the button (the same as on
  every other screen), and the dose, settings and brew time stay as typed.
- The bag or recipe deleted meanwhile (another device): "This bean or recipe isn't here
  any more. Go back and choose again."
- A bag marked finished meanwhile changes nothing: the brew saves to that bag.

**Home (changed):** after a save, a muted "Brew saved." under the Brew button. It goes
away when he leaves the screen or reloads.

**History (`#/coffee/history`, replacing "not built yet"):**

- Heading **History** and **Back**. Newest brew first, one row each, at least 48px tall:
  the recipe name in the serif, and a muted line "Today · 7:42 AM · V60 · Ethiopia Guji ·
  18 g" ("Yesterday", then "Mon, Nov 16", and "Nov 16, 2025" for older years).
- After 50 rows, a quiet **Show older brews** that adds the next 50.
- No brews yet: "No brews yet." Loading fails: "Can't load your brews right now." with
  **Try again**.

**A brew (`#/coffee/history/<id>`):**

- **Back** (to History), the recipe name as the heading, then muted: "Mon, Nov 16 ·
  7:42 AM". A deleted recipe's name shows as it was saved; a renamed one shows its new
  name.
- **Bean:** name, roaster, "9 days after roast" (left out without a roast date).
  **Method:** its name.
- **Settings**, one per line, label muted and value in tabular numbers: Dose 18 g, Water
  270 g, Ratio 1:15, Grinder, Grind size, Temperature, Bloom, Brew time. Blanks left out.
- **Pours and steps** as on the brew screen ("135 g", "+108", "at 0:45"), smaller.
- Two quiet links at the bottom: **Fix this brew** and **Delete brew** ("Delete this
  brew? This can't be undone." with **Delete** and **Cancel**, on the page itself).
- **Fix this brew** turns the settings into boxes (decision 3), labels above, the
  same sentences for problems as the brew screen, the bag as a short list of bags in
  use plus this brew's own bag. **Save** and **Cancel**. Water and pours follow a
  changed dose or ratio.
- Unknown address: "This brew isn't here any more." with **Back**.

**Desktop (wider than about 1024px):** History becomes two columns: the list on the left,
the chosen brew on the right; the chosen row is marked in copper. With none chosen, the
right side says "Choose a brew." in muted text. Phone: list and brew are separate screens.

**Deleting things (changed):** **Delete bean** on a bean with brews, and **Remove** on a
method with recipes or brews, show the sentences in decision 1 and nothing is
deleted. **Delete recipe** now asks "Delete this recipe? Brews made with it stay in your
history. This can't be undone."

## Data

All in the online database (Supabase), in one new table made and locked by migration
`004`. Only he, signed in, can read or change it.

- **brews:** when it was made (set by the database on saving), which bag, which method,
  which recipe (a link that is forgotten if the recipe is deleted), the recipe's name as
  it was, dose, ratio, grinder, grind size, temperature, bloom, brew time, and the pours
  and steps list (same shape as a recipe's). Ratings join in 1.7.
- **Copied, not pointed at:** every setting is stored on the brew itself, so editing a
  recipe never rewrites a past brew.
- **Worked out, not saved:** water (dose times ratio, as on recipes), days after roast
  (from the bag's roast date and the brew's day, so correcting a roast date corrects
  every brew).
- A bag or method with brews can't be deleted (database rule, decision 1).
- Each row carries his user number, filled in by the database. Nothing goes in browser
  storage, so no Export/Import here; brews join the backup file in `1.9-backups.md`.

### 2. Migration 004 and hand batch A

Write `supabase/migrations/004-brews.sql`:

```sql
-- Lets a brew point at a bean or recipe AND the same owner in one rule.
alter table beans add constraint beans_id_owner unique (id, user_id);
alter table recipes add constraint recipes_id_owner unique (id, user_id);

create table brews (
  id uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  user_id uuid not null default auth.uid() references auth.users on delete cascade,
  brewed_at timestamptz not null default now(),
  bean_id uuid not null,
  method_id uuid not null,
  recipe_id uuid,
  recipe_name text not null check (char_length(recipe_name) between 1 and 60),
  dose_grams numeric(4,1) not null check (dose_grams between 1 and 100),
  ratio numeric(3,1) not null check (ratio between 1 and 30),
  grinder text check (char_length(grinder) <= 60),
  grind_size text check (char_length(grind_size) <= 30),
  temperature integer check (temperature between 40 and 212),
  bloom_seconds integer check (bloom_seconds between 1 and 600),
  brew_seconds integer check (brew_seconds between 1 and 3600),
  steps jsonb not null default '[]'::jsonb
    check (jsonb_typeof(steps) = 'array' and jsonb_array_length(steps) <= 20),
  -- "no action": a bean or method with brews can't be deleted (decision 1).
  foreign key (bean_id, user_id) references beans (id, user_id) on delete no action,
  foreign key (method_id, user_id) references methods (id, user_id) on delete no action,
  -- Deleting a recipe keeps its brews and forgets only the link (decision 2).
  foreign key (recipe_id, user_id) references recipes (id, user_id)
    on delete set null (recipe_id)
);
create index brews_newest on brews (user_id, brewed_at desc);
create index brews_by_bean on brews (bean_id, user_id);
create index brews_by_method on brews (method_id, user_id);
create index brews_by_recipe on brews (recipe_id, user_id);
alter table brews enable row level security;
create policy "own brews only" on brews
  for all to authenticated
  using (user_id = auth.uid())
  with check (user_id = auth.uid());

grant select, insert, update, delete on brews to authenticated;
```

Hand batch A (click-by-click, `.claude/skills/click-by-click/SKILL.md`): **SQL Editor →
New query**, paste the file, **Run**.

*Check:* "Success. No rows returned", and **Table Editor** lists `brews` without an "RLS
disabled" or "Unrestricted" warning. If it reports a syntax error near `(recipe_id)`, the
project's Postgres is older than 15 (Supabase projects have been 15 or newer since 2023):
stop and bring the exact message back rather than changing the line.

### 3. Rules (pure functions, tested)

- `src/coffee/types.ts` (add): `Brew` matching the table, hand-written; `steps` is
  `RecipeStep[]`; `numeric` columns arrive as numbers.
- `src/coffee/brewLog.ts`:
  - `brewToSave(draft, recipeName, brewTimeText)`: the values to insert, copied from the

  - `brewToFixForm(brew)` and `checkBrewFix(form)`: the boxes in decision 3, checked
    with `checkDose`, `checkRatio`, `checkTemperature`, `checkShortText` and

  - `brewDayLabel(brewedAt, now)`: "Today · 7:42 AM", "Yesterday · 7:42 AM", "Mon, Nov 16
    · 7:42 AM", and "Nov 16, 2025 · 7:42 AM" in an earlier year. Local calendar days,
    fixed `en-US` wording so tests don't depend on the device.
  - `roastAgeOnBrewDay(roastDate, brewedAt)`: `daysSinceRoast` from 1.2 with the brew's
    local day as "today"; "9 days after roast", "Roast day", "1 day after roast"; nothing
    without a roast date or if the roast date is after the brew.
  - `historyRows(brews, beans, methods, recipes, now)`: each brew with its day label, bean
    and method names, and recipe name (the linked recipe's current name, or the saved

## Notes

- **One `brews` table with copied settings**, rejected: pointing at the recipe's numbers
  (editing a recipe would rewrite history, which the outline forbids) and a separate
  settings table (always read and saved with the brew).
- **Recipe name copied too**, so a deleted recipe's brews still read sensibly. While the
  link exists the current name wins, so renaming "Kasuya" doesn't split his history.
- **Method stored on the brew**, not taken from the recipe: a recipe can be moved to
  another method or deleted, and the brew must still say what it was made with.
- **`on delete set null (recipe_id)`** names the one column to clear; plain `set null`
  would also try to clear `user_id` and fail. It needs Postgres 15 or newer (step 2).
- **`no action`, not `restrict`**, for beans and methods, as in 1.3: `restrict` would
  block deleting his account. On saving a brew, code `23503` means the bag or recipe it
  points at has gone, so the same `isInUseError` check gets a different sentence there.
- **Water and days after roast worked out, not saved**, as 1.3 does for recipes. Rejected
  1.4's hint to save water: dose and ratio already fix it, and a fixed dose then can't
  leave a stale water figure. A corrected roast date fixes every brew's age.
- **The recipe's target time isn't copied**; `brew_seconds` is what it actually took.
  The box starts blank, rejected: filling it with the target, which would log a time he
  never measured. 1.6's timer fills this box.
- **Brew time and settings checked by 1.3's functions**, never new ones, so the brew
  screen, the fix form and the recipe page always agree.
- **History pages of 50 with "Show older brews"**, rejected: loading everything (Supabase
  returns at most 1000 rows per request by default, so "everything" would quietly stop
  at 1000) and endless scrolling (more code, nothing gained).
- **Names joined in the site** from the method, bean and recipe lists (small, already
  loaded the same way elsewhere), rejected: Supabase's nested selects, which make the
  hand-written types harder to keep right.
- **One route with an optional `:brewId?` and Tailwind-only two columns**, rejected: two
  routes (the list would reload on every tap on desktop) and a screen-size hook.
- **Fix in place on the brew's page**, rejected: a separate fix page (one more address,
  and on desktop it would leave the two-column view).
- **Finishing a bag doesn't touch an open brew screen** (1.4 asked): the brew still saves
  to that bag, which also covers the last scoop from a bag marked finished a bit early.
- **The draft isn't lifted into a shared holder:** saving happens on the brew screen
  itself. 1.6 lifts it if the timer becomes its own screen.
- **Rejected for now:** filtering History by bean, method or recipe (1.7), brew notes
  (1.7's rating note), changing a brew's date.
- **Split:** cups left and cost per cup moved to `1.10-bean-supply.md` (400-line cap).
- **For later plans:**
  - 1.6: the timer fills the **Brew time** box on the brew screen (`brew_seconds`). If the
    timer is a separate screen, lift `BrewDraft` and the brew time into a small React
    context around the coffee routes (not browser storage), and save from wherever **Save
    brew** ends up, still through `brewToSave`.
  - 1.7: rating columns go on `brews` in migration `005-…`; the "rate me" card goes on
    home next to the "Brew saved." line; History rows and `BrewDetails` have room for the
    dots and note. Filtering History is 1.7's.
  - 1.8: "repeat my latest brew" can build its draft with `draftFromBrew`; rank by the
    brew's own `method_id` (a recipe may have moved methods since); brews whose recipe
    was deleted have `recipe_id` null and count for no recipe. Any average over many
    brews must not fetch rows past Supabase's 1000-row limit.
  - 1.9: the backup file includes `brews`; on import, bags, methods and recipes must go
    in before the brews that point at them.
  - 1.10: dose sums over `brews.dose_grams` per `bean_id`; fetch only bags in use (the
    1000-row limit again).
  - Next migration is `005-…`.
