# Plan: Setting up a brew

**Status:** building: code done 2026-11-18, not pushed; waiting on migrations 002 and 003 (see "Build progress")
**Outline:** `docs/outlines/coffee-tracker.md`, sections "The first version" (Dose and water, Choosing a brew), "How it works", "Rules and worked example"
**Depends on:** `1.3-recipes.md` (must be built first: recipes table, `recipeMath.ts`,
`clock.ts`, `recipeForm.ts`, `recipeList.ts`), and through it `1.2-methods-and-beans.md`
and `1.1-look-and-sign-in.md`

## Goal

Make the big **Brew** button on the home screen do something. He taps it, picks the
method, sees his active bean already chosen (and can switch bags), and taps one of that
method's recipes. The next screen is what he looks at while standing at the kettle: the
coffee dose (the recipe's, changeable with big + and − buttons), the total water worked
out from it, and each pour as a large "pour to" weight for the scale with the single
amount small beside it. Changing the dose rescales the water and every pour at once. He
can also change the grinder, grind size, temperature, ratio or bloom **for today only**;
the saved recipe never changes. Nothing is saved in this plan: saving the brew is 1.5,
the timer 1.6, and "repeat" and "recommended" are 1.8.

## Decisions (settled)

Answered 2026-11-18: every recommendation below was approved, and the plan follows it.

1. **Switching bags on the Brew screen.** Recommended: the switch is **for this brew
   only**; the active bean stays as it is, and he changes it on the Beans screen with
   **Use**. That way brewing a friend's bag once doesn't change his default. The
   alternative: picking a bag here also makes it the active bean (one less step when he
   opens a new bag, but a one-off brew quietly changes his default). (Steps 4, 5.)
2. **Must a brew have a bean?** Recommended: **yes**. Recipes can't be tapped until a bean
   is chosen ("Choose a bean first."). Every brew in the log (1.5) then has a bean for
   "cups left", cost per cup and ratings by bean. The alternative allows a brew with no
   bean, which 1.5 would then have to log as "unknown bean". (Steps 4, 5.)
3. **What can be changed for today?** Recommended: dose, ratio, grinder, grind size,
   temperature and bloom. **Pours and steps are not changed here**: rescaling them by
   dose happens by itself, and a different pour pattern is a different recipe (save it as
   one). The alternative, editing pours on the brew screen, doubles the recipe form on the
   screen he uses most. (Step 5.)
4. **Dose buttons step by 0.5 g.** Recommended: + and − change the dose by **0.5 g**;
   tapping the number lets him type any dose such as 18.3. The alternative is 1 g steps
   (fewer taps for big changes, but no 18.5 without typing). (Steps 3, 5.)

## What he sees

**Choose a brew (`#/coffee/brew`, replacing "not built yet"):**

- Heading **Brew**, a **Back** link to home.
- **Bean** first: one quiet row with the active bean's name in the serif and its roast
  age muted underneath ("Roasted 9 days ago"), and a small **Change** on the right.
  Tapping **Change** opens a short list, right there, of the bags in use (finished bags
  are never shown), active bag first; tapping one picks it and closes the list.
  - No active bean: the row reads "No bean chosen" and the list is already open.
  - No bags in use at all: "Add a bean first." with an **Add bean** link to the bean page.
- **Method:** one tap-button per method, the chosen one in copper. Already chosen when he
  has only one method; otherwise nothing is chosen until he taps.
  - No methods at all: "Add a brewing method first." with a **Brewing methods** link, and
    nothing else below.
- **Recipe:** once a method is chosen, its recipes A to Z, each row the recipe name in the
  serif with the muted numbers under it ("1:15 · 18 g · 3 pours"), rows at least 48px
  tall. Tapping one opens the brew screen. No bean chosen: the rows are greyed with
  "Choose a bean first." above them (decision 2).
  - A method with no recipes: "No recipes for V60 yet." with an **Add recipe** link.
- Loading fails: "Can't load your coffee things right now." with **Try again**.

**The brew screen (`#/coffee/brew/<recipe>/<bean>`):**

- **Back** (to Choose a brew), then the recipe name as the heading and a muted line
  under it: "V60 · Ethiopia Guji · Roasted 9 days ago".
- **Dose:** the biggest thing on the screen: "18 g" in large tabular numbers between a
  large **−** and **+** (each at least 56px). Tapping the number turns it into a box on
  the decimal keypad; leaving the box keeps what he typed. A dose outside 1 to 100 g or
  with two decimals shows one plain line under it ("Dose should be 1 to 100 g, like 18 or
  18.5.") and the water and pours show "–" until it is fixed.
- **Water 270 g** right under it, in large numbers.
- **Pours and steps**, in the recipe's order, one row each:
  - A pour: the running total large ("**135 g**"), with the single amount small and
    muted beside it ("+108"), and "at 0:45" muted on the right when the recipe has a
    time. The last pour is always exactly the water.
  - A step: its words in normal text ("Open the valve"), with its time if any.
  - No pours (a Robot recipe): this list shows only the steps, or nothing.
- **Today's settings:** one muted line summing them up, for example "Comandante · 18
  clicks · 93 °C · 1:15 · bloom 0:45" (blanks left out), and a quiet **Change for
  today** link. Tapping it opens boxes for ratio, grinder, grind size, temperature and
  bloom, filled from the recipe, labels above the boxes. A box he has changed shows the
  recipe's value small beneath it ("Recipe: 18 clicks"). **Use recipe settings** puts
  them all back. Problems show one plain line under the box, the same sentences as on
  the recipe page. Changing the ratio rescales water and pours at once.
- The recipe's target time, muted: "Target 3:00", when the recipe has one.
- At the bottom, a muted line: "Saving brews and the timer come next." (1.5 replaces it
  with its button.)
- Unknown recipe or bean in the address: "This recipe isn't here any more." (or "This
  bean…") with **Back**. A bean marked finished since still shows; the address came
  from somewhere he chose.
- Loading fails: "Can't load this recipe right now." with **Try again**.

**Desktop:** the same screens in the centred column from 1.1.

**Nothing is saved:** leaving the brew screen forgets today's changes. Coming back to it
starts again from the recipe. Said plainly in Notes for 1.5.

## Data

- **Nothing new is saved, and no new tables.** No migration in this plan, so the next
  migration number stays `004-…`.
- The two screens **read** methods, beans and recipes through the existing data files
  from 1.2 and 1.3; those tables are already locked to him alone.
- Today's dose and changes live only on the open screen (not in browser storage), so no
  Export/Import is needed here.
- The address carries which recipe and which bean (their database ids, which mean
  nothing to anyone else); refreshing the brew screen reloads both and starts again from
  the recipe's settings.

## Steps

### 1. Pull and confirm 1.3 is built

Pull `main`. Confirm `src/coffee/recipesData.ts`, `src/coffee/recipeMath.ts`,
`src/coffee/clock.ts`, `src/coffee/recipeForm.ts`, `src/coffee/recipeList.ts`,
`src/coffee/beanList.ts`, `src/coffee/roastAge.ts` and `src/lib/useLoad.ts` exist. If
not, stop: 1.3 (and 1.2) come first.

*Check:* `npm run check` passes before any change.

### 2. Share the single-box checks from the recipe form

The brew screen must accept and refuse exactly what the recipe page does. In
`src/coffee/recipeForm.ts`, make sure these are exported functions of their own (if 1.3
wrote them inside `checkRecipe`, move them out and have `checkRecipe` call them):

- `checkRatio(text)`, `checkDose(text)`: number, at most one decimal, in range.
- `checkTemperature(text)`: whole number in the `TEMP_UNIT` range; blank is "not given".
- `checkShortText(text, max)`: trimmed; blank is "not given"; for grinder (60) and grind
  size (30).
- Bloom uses `parseClock` from `clock.ts` as it is.

Each returns the clean value or the sentence. Also export the one-decimal number
formatting that `recipeSummary` uses ("15", "15.5") as `formatOneDecimal(n)` in
`src/coffee/recipeList.ts`, if it isn't already.

*Check:* `src/coffee/recipeForm.test.ts` and `recipeList.test.ts` still pass unchanged,
plus the new cases in Testing.

### 3. Rules (pure functions, tested)

`src/coffee/brewSetup.ts`, using `totalWater` and `pourTargets` from `recipeMath.ts`
(never a second scaling sum):

- `BrewDraft` (hand-written type, also for 1.5 and 1.6): `methodId`, `beanId`,
  `recipeId`, `dose`, `ratio`, `grinder`, `grindSize`, `temperature`, `bloomSeconds`,
  `totalSeconds`, `steps` (the recipe's `RecipeStep` list, copied, unchanged). Blanks are
  `null`.
- `draftFromRecipe(recipe, beanId)`: a draft with every setting copied from the recipe,
  the dose being the recipe's `dose_grams`.
- `brewRows(draft)`: water rounded to whole grams, and the steps in order, each pour
  given its running total and single amount from `pourTargets` (percents of the pours
  only, in order), each action step kept with its text; every row keeps its `at`.
- `stepDose(dose, direction)`: plus or minus `DOSE_STEP` (0.5, decision 4), kept
  within 1 to 100, rounded to one decimal (so 18.3 + 0.5 is exactly 18.8).
- `changedSettings(draft, recipe)`: which of ratio, grinder, grind size, temperature and
  bloom differ from the recipe, with the recipe's value for the "Recipe: …" line.
- `settingsSummary(draft)`: the muted line, blanks left out, using `TEMP_UNIT`,
  `formatClock` and `formatOneDecimal`.
- `brewChoices(methods, beans, recipes)`: the method chosen by default (only when there
  is exactly one), the bean chosen by default (the active one, never a finished one),
  the bags for the picker (`splitFinished` then `sortBeans` from 1.2, in-use only), and
  recipes grouped by method with `groupRecipes` from 1.3.

*Check:* `npx vitest run src/coffee/brewSetup.test.ts` passes.

### 4. Choose a brew screen

`src/coffee/BrewChooseScreen.tsx` at `#/coffee/brew`, **replacing** the 1.1 "not built
yet" route (don't add a second one). Loads methods, beans and recipes together through
`useLoad` (`methodsData` list, `beansData` list all, `recipesData` list all), then
`brewChoices`. The default bean is the active one, the same bag `getActiveBean()` gives;
it is taken from the list already loaded rather than asking the database twice.

- The chosen bean and method are screen state. Picking a bag here doesn't change the
  active bean (decision 1).
- A recipe row is a link to `#/coffee/brew/<recipe id>/<bean id>`; with no bean chosen,
  rows are not links (decision 2).
- Roast age with `daysSinceRoast` and `roastAgeLabel` from `roastAge.ts`.
- Load failures go through `dataMessage` and `ErrorLine`.

*Check:* on `npm run dev`, with two methods, two bags in use, one finished bag and three
recipes: the active bag is pre-chosen, the finished bag never appears in **Change**,
tapping a method lists only its recipes A to Z, and a recipe opens the brew screen with
both ids in the address.

### 5. The brew screen

`src/coffee/BrewScreen.tsx` at `#/coffee/brew/:recipeId/:beanId` (a new route next to
the one above). Loads the recipe (`recipesData` get one), the bean (`beansData` get one)
and the methods list (for the method's name) through `useLoad`, then
`draftFromRecipe`.

- The dose, today's settings boxes and the draft are screen state. The draft is rebuilt
  from what is typed: each box through its check from step 2; the water and rows come
  from `brewRows` only while dose and ratio are valid.
- **−** and **+** call `stepDose` and are disabled at 1 and 100. The dose number is a
  button that becomes an `inputmode="decimal"` box (16px text or more, 48px tall).
- **Change for today** reveals the boxes; `changedSettings` drives the "Recipe: …"
  lines; **Use recipe settings** resets the draft to `draftFromRecipe`.
- Numbers use tabular figures; the running totals use the large size, single amounts
  small and muted; colours by job name only.
- Unknown ids (the get returns nothing) show the "isn't here any more" sentences.

*Check:* on `npm run dev`, open the worked-example recipe (18 g, 1:15, shares 10, 40,
50): water 270 g, pours to 27, 135, 270 with +27, +108, +135. Tap **+** four times:
20 g, water 300 g, pours 30, 150, 300. Change the ratio to 16 for today: water 320 g.
Open the recipe page: it still says 1:15 and 18 g.

### 6. Publish

Run `npm run check`, commit (files staged by name), push `main`.

*Check:* the GitHub **Actions** run is green; on the live site the Brew button opens
Choose a brew.

## Notes

- **Two screens, not a three-step wizard** (rejected: method, then bean, then recipe as
  separate pages). Bean, method and recipe fit on one phone screen, most brews are
  "active bean, the usual method, tap a recipe", and each extra page is a tap and a Back.
- **Bean before method on Choose a brew:** the outline says the bean is already chosen,
  so it reads as a fact at the top, and the method and recipe below are the taps.
- **Recipe and bean in the address** (`#/coffee/brew/<recipe>/<bean>`), rejected: keeping
  the choice only in memory. A refresh, or the phone reloading the tab after sleeping,
  then still lands on the right brew screen. Path parts rather than `?bean=`: both are
  always needed, and route parameters are the plainest way to read them.
- **Today's changes kept on the screen only**, rejected: browser storage (AGENTS.md would
  then need Export/Import for a few minutes' state, and a stale draft could reappear
  days later) and a shared app-wide holder (nothing in 1.4 needs it yet). The cost: a
  refresh mid-brew returns to the recipe's settings. **For 1.5/1.6:** when logging or the
  timer need the draft on another screen, lift it into a small React context around the
  coffee routes rather than storage; 1.6 already keeps the screen awake.
- **Active bean read from the bean list** instead of a second call to `getActiveBean()`:
  the picker needs the list anyway, and both use the same `is_active` flag.
- **Checks shared with the recipe page** (step 2), rejected: new checks for the brew
  screen. Two copies of "dose 1 to 100, one decimal" would drift apart.
- **Water rounded to whole grams**, matching `pourTargets`, whose last pour is exactly the
  rounded water: the water line and the last pour can never disagree.
- **Rejected for now:** remembering the last method chosen (1.8's "repeat my latest
  brew" covers it with real data), editing pours on the brew screen (decision 3),
  a "save as new recipe" button (not asked for), cups left on the bean row (1.10).
- **For later plans:**
  - `BrewDraft` and `draftFromRecipe` in `src/coffee/brewSetup.ts` are what a brew is
    built from. 1.5 copies the draft's settings into the brew it saves (plus water from
    `brewRows`), replaces the "Saving brews and the timer come next." line with its
    button, and decides whether finishing a bag affects an open brew screen.
  - 1.6 builds its steps from `brewRows(draft)`: running totals are already worked out.
  - 1.8 adds "Repeat" and "Recommended" above the recipe list on Choose a brew, and can
    open the brew screen with a dose and grind other than the recipe's; it will need
    `draftFromRecipe` to accept those (or a draft built from the last brew).
  - Bean picking here doesn't change the active bean (decision 1); if he answers
    otherwise, the picker calls `setActiveBean` from 1.2.
  - Next migration is still `004-…`.
