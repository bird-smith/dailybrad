# Start here

This is a starter kit for building your own website with AI, without being a programmer. It's
adapted from the setup Noah used to build a real site, without being a programmer either. It
costs $0 to start.

**What you get:**

- A small working website that publishes itself to the internet.
- Instructions that tell any AI tool how to work with a non-programmer.
- A step-by-step way of working that stops the AI from building the wrong thing.
- A draft plan for your first feature: a coffee tracker to help you dial in your grind, scoops
  and water.

## Good to know up front

- **Your repo is public.** GitHub's free website hosting requires it. Anyone can read your code
  and plans, so keep private things out of these files. Your coffee entries aren't stored
  here: they stay in your own browser.
- **You'll hit a limit on the free AI.** It's expected, and walkthrough 3 covers what to do.
- **Noah has offered to do some of the heavy lifting.** You decide what you want, in an outline and
  plan stubs, which is the light part and easy to do for free. Noah can then use his paid Claude
  account to write, improve and build some of those plans. See Part 6 of the setup walkthrough.
- **You don't need to understand the code.** You do need to read the outlines and plans
  carefully, because that's where you steer.
